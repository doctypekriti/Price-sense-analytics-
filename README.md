# PriceSense: SQL-Driven Pricing Intelligence Framework

## Overview

PriceSense is a data analytics and business intelligence project focused on pricing optimization, customer segmentation, and revenue growth. The project leverages SQL-based analysis on transactional, demographic, and geographic datasets to identify hidden business opportunities and generate actionable pricing recommendations.

The objective was to transform raw sales data into strategic insights that can improve profitability without acquiring new customers.

---

## Key Highlights

- Analyzed 48,000+ transaction records using SQL
- Performed customer segmentation based on purchasing behavior
- Identified pricing inefficiencies across products and customer groups
- Conducted geographic and demographic analysis
- Evaluated revenue opportunities through tier-based pricing
- Estimated ₹13–14 Lakhs annual revenue uplift through strategic recommendations

---

## Problem Statement

Businesses often apply uniform pricing strategies across different customer segments and geographic regions. This project aims to determine:

- Which products are underpriced or overpriced
- Which customer groups contribute the most revenue
- Whether pricing varies effectively across regions
- How revenue can be increased through targeted pricing strategies

## Key Findings

### Revenue Opportunity
A significant concentration of sales was found in lower-priced products, indicating strong upselling potential.

### Geographic Price Gap
Customer willingness-to-pay varied across city tiers, revealing opportunities for differentiated pricing strategies.

### Customer Behavior
Premium customer segments were not generating proportionally higher revenue despite their intended positioning.

### Channel Performance
Certain sales channels demonstrated significantly higher average order values than others.

## Business Impact

The proposed pricing and segmentation strategies have the potential to generate:

- ₹4.8 Lakhs through premium SKU migration

without acquiring additional customers.
